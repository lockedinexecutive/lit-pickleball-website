Frequently Asked Questions

Can travellers or tourists join sessions? +

Absolutely! We cater specifically to expats and travellers. You don't need Zalo to contact us—just use WhatsApp. We'll get you on the court quickly.

Can I borrow a racket? +

Yes, high-quality demo rackets are available for free during your first session. Rental is also available for a small fee for subsequent sessions.

What are the payment methods? +

We accept Cash (VND/USD), Bank Transfer, Credit Card, and Apple Pay. A small deposit is required to secure your booking.

What is the cancellation policy? +

We require 12 hours notice for cancellations. Late cancellations or no-shows will forfeit the deposit. Rain? No problem, we are fully covered.

I have no experience. What level should I join? +

Start with our "Learn" program or book a 1-on-1 assessment. We separate groups strictly by level (2.0 to 4.0+) to ensure everyone gets the most out of their time.
