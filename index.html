Level 01 · Train

LIT Training Track

10 Sessions. One Clear Development Plan.

Whether you are holding a paddle for the first time or already playing regularly, your LIT journey begins with understanding your current game, your goals and what you need to improve next.

Every Training Track Includes

  • 10 coaching sessions
  • 60 minutes per session
  • Covered court included
  • Coaching conducted in English
  • RPO Level 1 certified coaches
  • Maximum ratio of 4 players per coach
  • Paddle rental available at the venue
  • Initial assessment
  • Personal development focus
  • Match-play application
  • Final reassessment and next-step recommendation

Paddle is not included.

For New and Early-Stage Players

Start with Confidence.

Learn the fundamentals in a structured environment where questions are welcome, mistakes are expected and no previous experience is required.

  • Rules and scoring
  • Grip and ready position
  • Serve and return
  • Ball contact and control
  • Movement & rally consistency
  • Doubles positioning & community-play etiquette
Choose Start

For Players Who Already Know the Basics

Already Play? Start at Your Level.

Your first session identifies what is already working, what is holding your game back and where the next ten sessions should focus.

  • Technical consistency
  • Serve and return quality
  • Shot control & selection
  • Transition movement & court positioning
  • Doubles communication & decision-making
  • Match-play patterns & individual weaknesses

The exact development focus may differ between participants.

Assess

  • Understand current ability
  • Identify goals
  • Confirm the appropriate track
  • Establish first development priorities
02

Build

Sessions 3–5

  • Develop control and consistency
  • Correct inefficient habits
  • Practise repeatable movement and shot patterns
03

Apply

Sessions 6–8

  • Use new skills in live points
  • Improve positioning and decisions
  • Learn to play with different partners
04

Play Forward

Sessions 9–10

  • Match-play application
  • Reassessment
  • Identify the next development goal
  • Coach recommendation for next step
