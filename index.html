FAQ

Only for expats?+

No. LIT is for locals, expats, travellers, students and working adults who can communicate in English or want to practise English through sport.

Never played before?+

Yes. Beginners can enquire about Limited-spot group training or private 1:1 coaching so the team can recommend the right starting point.

Paddle included?+

No. Players should bring their own paddle or rent one directly at the venue.

Court fee included?+

Yes. The covered court is included in the coaching session.

Session length?+

All coaching and community sessions run for 60 minutes.

How to pay?+

Payment is made by bank transfer after the booking has been confirmed.

Can travellers join?+

Yes. Travellers can enquire about available coaching sessions through WhatsApp.

How Membership works?+

Members receive access to two hosted community sessions each week, on Tuesday and Friday from 4:00–5:00 PM, plus an included LIT shirt and Friday post-game beer for members aged 18+.
