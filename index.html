FAQ

Do I need any pickleball experience?+

No. Complete beginners should select the Start Track.

What if I already know how to play?+

Select the Improve Track. Your first session will help identify the appropriate development focus so you do not repeat material you already understand.

Do I need to know my rating?+

No. LIT will help identify a suitable starting point.

Can I purchase only one coaching session?+

No. LIT coaching is delivered as a complete 10-session pack to support consistency and visible progress.

Will I have the same coach for all ten sessions?+

LIT aims to maintain coaching continuity whenever possible. Both coaches work from the same core framework and hold equal coaching status.

Is one coach for beginners and the other for advanced?+

No. Start and Improve describe player tracks, not coach hierarchy. Both LIT coaches can work with players at either entry point.

Is a paddle included?+

No. Players may bring their own paddle or rent one at the venue.

What happens after ten sessions?+

The coach provides a reassessment and recommends the appropriate next step: continued training, another development focus, community application or waitlist.

Can experienced players apply directly for Membership?+

Yes. Community-ready players may apply directly and may be invited to a short level and community-fit assessment.

Is membership automatically included with coaching?+

No. Coaching and Community Membership are separate products.

How do I pay?+

Payment is made by bank transfer after enrolment has been confirmed.
