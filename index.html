FAQ

Questions, Answered

Do I need experience to join coaching?+

Not at all — built for beginners and existing players alike.

If I complete a coaching pack, am I guaranteed a Membership spot?+

No. Coaching and Membership are independent — one doesn't guarantee the other.

I already play — can I apply for Membership without coaching?+

Yes — experienced players can apply directly, no coaching pack required.

Can I buy a single coaching session?+

No — only sold as a 10-session pack, group or private.

How does Membership selection work?+

Curated through a waitlist — LIT reaches out when a spot opens.

Where is LIT Pickleball located?+
