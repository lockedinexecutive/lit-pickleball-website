Our Mission

LIT Pickleball was founded to provide a structured, professional, and genuinely fun environment for pickleball players in Ho Chi Minh City. Whether you've never held a paddle or you're looking to refine your competitive edge, our RPO-certified coaches are here to help you level up.

We believe in small ratios (max 4:1), fixed courts, and transparent pricing so you always know what you're getting. No chasing games, no hidden fees. Just show up and play.
